One take, no cuts

Three TV episodes shot as a single unbroken shot, in the order they aired, and what the long take does for each one.

By Tomoyoshi Takita · September 19, 2026 · 3 min read

Format
List
Picks
3
Aired
2022–2025
Spoilers
None

INT. COUCH - REMOTE IN HAND, NO PAUSING - SUNDAY AFTERNOON

A oner is a scene filmed in one continuous take, with no cuts. Movies have done it for decades. TV has started doing it for whole episodes, and the best ones use it to trap you in the room with the characters.

1. The Bear, “Review” (2022)

Season one, episode seven. Almost twenty minutes in a single shot, during a lunch rush that falls apart at a Chicago sandwich shop where everyone is already exhausted. The camera never gives you a place to rest. By the end you understand why Carmy cannot leave this job at the door. Start here. It is the shortest, and it changes how you watch the other two.

2. Adolescence (2025)

All four episodes of this Netflix limited series are single takes, each close to an hour long. Episode three is the one people talk about: a thirteen-year-old boy in a secure facility and the psychologist assigned to assess him, in one room, for the whole hour. Owen Cooper and Erin Doherty make it feel like a stage play you are not allowed to leave.

3. The Studio, “The Oner” (2025)

Season one, episode two. Seth Rogen plays a new studio head who visits a set where Sarah Polley, playing herself, is trying to get a complicated single-take shot before the sun goes down. He keeps ruining it. The episode is shot as one take too, so the joke works on two levels. It is the funniest of the three.

A cut gives you a breath. Take the cuts away and you hold it with them.

Why it works on TV

A kitchen in the middle of service, an interview room, and a film set racing the sunset are all places where nobody can step away. The long take makes you stay too. Some oners hide a stitch in a fast pan or a dark doorway. You will not notice, and it does not matter.

Admit one · Verdict

Watch all three

Start with The Bear. It is twenty minutes long and makes the other two easier to read.
