Blackjack advisor is installed

It opens in Chrome's side panel, next to the page you're on. It can't see or touch that page.

  1. Pin it to the toolbar.

    Select the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to Blackjack Strategy Advisor.

  2. Open the side panel.

    Chrome didn't open the panel. Select the extension's icon in the toolbar instead.

  3. Pick the game, then type your two cards and the dealer's upcard.

    Pick Free Bet or Blackjack at the top of the panel. The play appears as soon as the third card is in. Keep the game in a normal tab, not full screen, so the panel stays beside it.
