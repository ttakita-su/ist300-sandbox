    Hand history

    Hands you look up are kept in this extension on this device, newest first, and all count as assisted. The panel only knows what you typed. It keeps your most recent rounds.

    About this panel

    Type the cards in a blackjack hand and the panel shows the basic-strategy play. It knows two games; pick one at the top of the Advisor tab, or press G.

    • Free Bet: Free Bet Blackjack, where some doubles and splits are on the house. The panel says when a play is free. It reads the same strategy file as the Free Bet trainer, so the two give the same answers.
    • Blackjack: regular blackjack, where every double and split costs a bet equal to your first.

    How to use it

    Type your two cards, then the dealer's upcard, then every card you're dealt after that. A card you type means you followed the last answer. If you did something else, press H, S, D or P first, or use the buttons under the keypad. To enter the dealer's card at any point, tap it in the dealer row or press U first.

    Keep the game in a normal tab, not full screen, so the panel stays beside it.

    What it never does

    The panel has no access to web pages. It can't see, click or bet on the casino page, and it makes no network requests. You make every play. It takes no bets and handles no money.

    Table rules it assumes:

    These follow the game picked on the Advisor tab.

      At a table with other rules, this advice may be wrong. Basic strategy lowers the house edge but doesn't remove it: under these rules. If you use the panel while you play, check that the site's terms allow strategy aids.

      Your data

      Hands are stored only in this extension's storage on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, there are no analytics, and no account or personal detail is ever asked for. Turn saving off or clear it in History, or remove the extension to delete everything.
