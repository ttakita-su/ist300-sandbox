Before your first hand

Same cards. A few new rules.

1
Your double can be free.

Double a two-card hard 9, 10, or 11 without risking another practice chip. Other two-card doubles use an extra original chip.

2
Your split can be free, too.

Split any pair except ten-value cards. The new hand uses a free chip, so its strategy can differ. Resplit up to four hands. Split aces receive one card, with resplitting allowed.

3
Dealer 22 is a push.

Busted hands still lose. Natural blackjack pays 3:2. The dealer hits soft 17, checks for blackjack, and surrender is unavailable.

After each decision, read the feedback and select Continue to reveal the next card. “Show best move” marks the hand assisted.
