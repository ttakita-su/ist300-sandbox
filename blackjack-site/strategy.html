Basic Strategy

Basic strategy is the mathematically optimal play for every combination of your hand and the dealer's upcard. It does not make blackjack a winning game, but it cuts the house edge to roughly half a percent — far better than playing on instinct.

Four Rules Worth Memorizing First

  • Always split aces and eights. Never split tens or fives.
  • Double on 11 against anything except an ace.
  • Stand on 12–16 when the dealer shows 2–6; hit when they show 7 or higher.
  • Never take insurance.

These charts assume a common multi-deck game where the dealer stands on soft 17 and doubling after a split is allowed. Exact house rules shift a few cells.
