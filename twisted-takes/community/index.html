Ground rules

  1. Discuss the work. Talk about the story, the craft and how it made you feel. Critique the movie, not the person who liked it.
  2. Respect other viewers. Taste is personal. No insults, slurs, pile-ons or gatekeeping about who is a “real” fan.
  3. Label spoilers. Put “Spoilers for [title]” before anything about endings, twists or late reveals, and give newer releases extra room.
  4. Keep it relevant. Stay on the movie, show or question at hand. No spam, self-promotion or sharing anyone’s personal information.

True crime titles involve real people and real losses. Please keep those conversations especially respectful of victims and their families.
