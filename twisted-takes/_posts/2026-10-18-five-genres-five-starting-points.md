Trying a genre you don't usually watch can feel like a gamble. Pick the wrong first title and you might write off the whole category.

Here's one starting point for each of our five launch genres: why it works as a way in, what kind of night it gives you, and how it compares with the other four, so you can choose tonight.

## Sci-Fi: Inception

[Inception]({{ '/reviews/inception/' | relative_url }}) is one big sci-fi idea with a heist movie built around it. Leonardo DiCaprio plays a thief who steals secrets from people's dreams, hired for a job that flips the usual task: plant an idea instead of taking one.

**Why it's a good door:** no spaceships or genre knowledge required. The film explains its own rules, then has fun bending them, and shows that sci-fi can be about how your mind works.

**The experience:** a phones-down movie. It's long, it stacks dream inside dream, and missing a scene can leave you lost. The intensity comes from chases, countdowns and a booming score, not fear. Watch it with friends who like to debate afterward.

**How it differs:** it asks for the most focus of the five.

## True Crime: The Tinder Swindler

[The Tinder Swindler]({{ '/reviews/the-tinder-swindler/' | relative_url }}) is a documentary about real people. Women interviewed in the film describe matching on Tinder with a man who presented himself as a wealthy, jet-setting businessman, and the film traces how those relationships turned into requests for money.

**Why it's a good door:** instead of a violent case, it focuses on deception, money and trust in a dating-app world many viewers know. You still get the genre's core tools: first-person interviews and a story assembled piece by piece.

**The experience:** easy to follow, with a medium attention level. Expect to talk back to the screen. Watch it with a roommate or friends, and remember these are real people retelling painful experiences.

**How it differs:** the only true story here. It's more frustrating than frightening.

## Fantasy: Spirited Away

[Spirited Away]({{ '/reviews/spirited-away/' | relative_url }}) is Hayao Miyazaki's hand-drawn animated film about Chihiro, a ten-year-old who, while moving with her parents, stumbles into a world of spirits and ends up working at a bathhouse that serves them.

**Why it's a good door:** it proves fantasy doesn't need a chosen one, a map or a ten-book backstory. You learn its strange rules at the same pace Chihiro does.

**The experience:** absorbing but gentle. A few spirits are genuinely eerie, but the mood leans curious more than scary. It suits a cozy night in, alone or with younger siblings.

**How it differs:** the only animated pick, and the most dreamlike. Inception builds dreams with rules; Spirited Away lets you wander.

## Rom-Com: 10 Things I Hate About You

[10 Things I Hate About You]({{ '/reviews/10-things-i-hate-about-you/' | relative_url }}) moves Shakespeare's *The Taming of the Shrew* into a late-’90s high school. Bianca can't date until her older sister Kat does, and Kat isn't interested in anyone, so a plan forms to pay Patrick, a classmate with a scary reputation, to take her out.

**Why it's a good door:** it has the rom-com basics down: a clear setup, quick banter and characters with strong opinions. Kat's sharp, outspoken attitude makes every argument fun to watch.

**The experience:** low effort, high fun. A comfort pick for a sleepover or a tired night.

**How it differs:** the lightest, lowest-stakes option here. Nothing is trying to scare or confuse you.

## Horror: A Quiet Place

[A Quiet Place]({{ '/reviews/a-quiet-place/' | relative_url }}) follows a family surviving in a world where creatures hunt anything that makes a sound, so they walk barefoot, sign instead of speaking and plan every move around silence. Emily Blunt and John Krasinski, who also directed, play the parents.

**Why it's a good door:** the scares come from suspense, not gore. Once you know the one rule, the smallest moments turn tense, and a family protecting each other gives you something to care about beyond the fear.

**The experience:** high intensity, but fairly short and easy to follow. Lights low, room quiet: you'll notice every crunch of your own snacks.

**How it differs:** the most intense of the five, and the one most affected by where you watch it.
