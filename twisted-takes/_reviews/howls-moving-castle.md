## Premise

Sophie is a quiet young hatmaker in a town bracing for war. When a spiteful witch curses her into the body of an old woman, she strikes out alone and lets herself into the walking castle of Howl, a wizard with an alarming reputation.

## The Take

Next to [Spirited Away]({{ '/reviews/spirited-away/' | relative_url }}), the story is looser and more knotted, but the film cares more about feelings than mechanics: being underestimated, making a home out of a mess, falling for someone flighty. On those terms, it mostly earns its two hours.

## What Works

The castle is wonderfully odd: a clanking heap of turrets and chimneys on spindly bird legs, wheezing as it lurches along. Sophie is a practical, plainspoken lead who fixes problems with a broom rather than a spell. Calcifer, the grumpy fire demon powering it all, gets the best laughs. A recurring waltz lifts even the chores, while dark airships give the war real weight.

## What May Divide Viewers

The second half gets tangled, and the film never fully explains its war or several spells. Readers of Diana Wynne Jones's novel will also spot big changes.

## Who Should Watch

Viewers who pick films by atmosphere, and anyone drawn to a cranky heroine with a hidden soft side.
