Good sci-fi hands you one strange rule and then asks what it does to people. What if a dream could be robbed? What if a planet's clock ran slower than the one back home? What if the hardest part of meeting aliens was the grammar? What if the life you know were not quite real?

Inception, Interstellar, Arrival and The Matrix all trust you to keep up. Where they split is temperature. Inception runs its puzzle like a heist, cool and precise. Interstellar goes huge and openly emotional about family across impossible distances. Arrival is quiet and patient, more interested in words than weapons. The Matrix is pure momentum, its ideas delivered at the speed of a fight. Pick the one that matches your mood tonight.
