Fantasy works best when a world feels lived in: the dusty corners, the odd rules, the food on the table. Each of these four films opens a door and trusts you to walk through it.

The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring is the long road, a quest that grows heavier and grander with every mile. Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone is the cozy first term, all train platforms, moving staircases and new friends. Spirited Away drops a ten-year-old into a bathhouse for spirits, where good manners and hard work count for more than swords. Howl's Moving Castle begins with a curse and a walking house, then cares more about kindness than about the war at its edges. Two are live-action, two are hand-drawn by Studio Ghibli, and all four reward a second visit.
