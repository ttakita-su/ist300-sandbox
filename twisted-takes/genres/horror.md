Horror is a safe place to practice being afraid. The danger is pretend, and you still check the hallway afterward. The best of it turns that fear toward something real: family, home, the people we trust.

All four of these films keep the threat close. The Conjuring is a classic haunted-house setup with a pair of paranormal investigators and a farmhouse that will not settle down. A Quiet Place makes silence a survival rule, so a dropped dish becomes an emergency. Get Out begins as an awkward weekend visit to a girlfriend's parents and lets the unease pile up. Hereditary is the slowest and heaviest, a grief story that crawls under your skin instead of jumping at you. Watch with the lights low and your phone in another room.
