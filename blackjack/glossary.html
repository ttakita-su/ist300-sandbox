Glossary of Terms

Common blackjack vocabulary, in plain language.

Blackjack (natural)
An ace and a 10-value card as your first two cards.
Bust
A hand total over 21. An automatic loss, even if the dealer busts afterward.
Push
A tie with the dealer. Your bet is returned.
Upcard
The dealer's face-up card. It drives nearly every strategy decision you make.
Hole card
The dealer's face-down card.
Hard hand
A hand with no ace, or where the ace must count as 1 to avoid busting.
Soft hand
A hand where an ace counts as 11 without busting. Cannot bust on the next card.
Double down
Doubling your bet in exchange for exactly one more card.
Split
Turning a pair into two separate hands with two separate bets.
Surrender
Forfeiting the hand for half your bet back, before taking any cards.
Insurance
A side bet that the dealer has blackjack when showing an ace. A bad bet.
Shoe
The box holding the multiple decks the dealer draws from.
House edge
The casino's long-run statistical advantage. Roughly 0.5% with perfect basic strategy, and several percent without it.
Basic strategy
The chart of mathematically optimal plays for every hand and upcard.
Card counting
Tracking the ratio of high to low cards remaining in the shoe to adjust bets. Legal, but casinos will ask you to leave.
