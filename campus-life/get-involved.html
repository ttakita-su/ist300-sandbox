A reasonable first semester

Pick one thing you're already good at
It gives you an immediate role and a reason people learn your name. Media, performance, a club sport, a cultural org — whatever you'd have joined in high school, join the bigger version.
Pick one thing you know nothing about
This is where the interesting version of college happens. The cost of trying is one meeting.
Go to one Dome event early
Not because you have to like sports, but because it's the fastest way to understand what people mean when they say the campus has a center.
Stop at two
Three organizations is how students end up doing none of them well. Depth is what turns an activity into a group of friends.

The one-sentence version

Syracuse gives you an unusually short distance between wanting to belong somewhere and actually belonging somewhere — a compact campus, a very long list of organizations, and one building big enough to put everyone in at once.

Organization lists, event calendars, and club contacts change every year — check the University's current Student Experience pages for what's active this semester.
