Traditions That Actually Get Used

A tradition only counts if students still show up for it.

The Case Traditions Get Involved

Every university has a founding date and a fight song. What separates a living campus culture from a plaque on a wall is whether the traditions are still load-bearing — whether people plan their week around them.

Otto the Orange

Syracuse's mascot is a citrus fruit, and it is undefeated. Otto has been the face of the University for more than 40 years and was inducted into the Mascot Hall of Fame in 2023. The genius of Otto is that he isn't intimidating — he's cheerful, absurd, and instantly recognizable, which is exactly the right energy for a student body rather than an army.

National Orange Day

Every March 24 — the anniversary of the University's founding in 1870 — the Orange community wears orange and turns out for community service. It's a tradition built around doing something, which is why it has survived. Alumni participate wherever they've ended up, so the campus tradition scales past campus.

The Crouse Chimes

First installed in 1889 and renovated in 1981, the chimes in Crouse College ring twice a day and again for special occasions. They are played by students. If you are on the Quad at the right hour, the soundtrack of your walk to class is being performed live by a classmate a few hundred feet above you — which is a strange and wonderful thing to get used to.

Marshall Street

"M Street" runs along the edge of campus and has been the student commercial strip for generations — food, coffee, shops, and a well-worn set of bars and pubs that students have been packing nightly for decades. It matters because it's adjacent. You don't need a car, a bus, or a plan. You need shoes.

Why proximity beats programming

Universities spend enormous effort scheduling events to build community. Syracuse's advantage is structural rather than programmed: the Dome, the Quad, Hendricks Chapel, the Barnes Center, and Marshall Street are all within a short walk of where students sleep. Traditions stick when the friction to participate is close to zero.
